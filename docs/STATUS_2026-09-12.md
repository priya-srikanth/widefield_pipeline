## RESOLVED — the independent measure is built and it confirms the falls

`epoch_15_evoked_maps_cue` (figure 15) renders per-position **post-cue minus PRE-CUE**, a
within-trial reference, so the six positions are **independent**. Acute amplitude against each
position's own pre-stroke value:

| near ipsi | near middle | near contra | far ipsi | **far middle** | **far CONTRA** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.21 | 1.14 | 1.22 | 0.83 | **0.40** | **0.11** |

**The falls survive and GROW once the shared reference is removed** — far-contra 0.47 → **0.11**,
far-middle 0.48 → **0.40**. The coupled measure was *understating* them, because far-contra's own
collapse dragged down the reference it was scored against.

**The "increases" shrink, as predicted** — near-middle 1.53 → **1.14**. The near positions do rise
modestly (1.14–1.22) and that is now an independent observation rather than an artefact, but it is
less than half what figure 14 implied.

**Recovery is ordered**: acute strongly negative, subacute near zero, chronic positive, for
far-contra and far-middle specifically. Read figure 15 first; figure 14 isolates what distinguishes
positions but couples them.

## RESOLVED -- the permutation test is fixed, and it now says something specific

Priya, of the old output: *"this contour of significance looks like total artifact ... hard to
believe nothing else is significant?"* Both halves were right and both were the SAME bug. Fixed in
`86a48e4`, pinned by `tests/test_cluster_permutation_mask.py` (7 tests).

**What was wrong, in four parts.**

1. **`t` was computed over the whole frame.** 138,387 of 345,600 pixels are not brain; there both
   the mean and the between-animal SD are ~0, so `|mean| / (se + 1e-12)` was unbounded. Clusters
   traced the image border. And because those clusters appeared in every PERMUTED draw too, they
   inflated the null's cluster-mass threshold past every interior effect -- the artefact faked one
   positive and hid the real ones with the same mechanism. Now `t` is identically 0 outside
   `beta_maps.brain_mask()`.
2. **The border bug has an interior twin.** Four animals agreeing by chance gives `se` ~ 0 and the
   same unbounded `t` inside the brain. Floored at the **25th percentile of the in-mask `se`**,
   recomputed per draw so the null stays exchangeable. Measured: the 1st percentile of in-mask `se`
   is exactly **0.0**; p5 still leaves max `t` = 31.8 against p25's 16.3, while the suprathreshold
   count barely moves (197,625 -> 195,482) -- so the floor is not what drives the result.
3. **The cluster-forming threshold was a constant.** `t = 2.0` is **p = 0.14** at df=3, and it
   showed: 18.8% of the brain cleared it for NEAR-IPSILATERAL, a position with no detectable
   change, against the ~14% a df=3 null predicts. It now comes from the df (3.18 at n=4), and the
   near positions drop to 1.0-7.1% while far-contra holds at 85.7%.
4. **Polarities were merged.** Thresholding `|t|` lets an increase and a touching decrease join
   into one cluster whose combined mass clears a bar neither would clear alone. Labelled apart now.

**The result, which was invisible before and is the point of the whole arm** (figure 15's
position-INDEPENDENT evoked maps, significant pixels per panel):

| position | acute | subacute | chronic |
|---|---|---|---|
| near ipsi | -- | -- | -- |
| near middle | -- | -- | -- |
| near contra | -- | -- | -- |
| far ipsi | -- | -- | -- |
| **far middle** | **96,138** | -- | -- |
| **far CONTRA** | **177,590** | **27,883** | -- |

Three things at once. **Only the two far positions ever reach significance.** **The four near
positions never do, in any epoch** -- so the "increases" figure 14 showed at near positions are not
merely uninterpretable through the coupling argument, they are also not there. And **the recovery
is ordered**: far-contra significant acutely AND subacutely, far-middle acutely only, neither
chronically. That is the encoder's story and the amplitude story with a test behind it.

**THE TEST CANNOT SEPARATE THE STROKE FROM ELAPSED TIME, and no version of it could.** Shuffling
epoch labels within animal tests exchangeability between epochs, but pre-stroke sessions all come
BEFORE post-stroke ones, so pre-vs-post is perfectly confounded with weeks of window clearing,
GCaMP expression and photobleaching. A monotonic drift in any of those is genuinely significant
here. **The defence is position specificity, not the p-value**: every one of those confounds acts on
all six positions at once and a ventrolateral striatal lesion does not, so what carries the claim is
that far-middle and far-contra reach significance while the four near positions never do, on the
same days through the same window. Read the CONTRAST between rows; a single row's contour is not
evidence that a stroke caused it.

**A cluster filling 86% of the mask is an answer, not a failure of the test.** A position the
animal has stopped attempting loses its whole task-evoked response, not a piece of it. The
informative contrast is against the near positions on the same figure, which produce nothing.

## RESOLVED -- the vessel structure in the POST maps is not residual haemodynamics

`wfield_local/hemo_map_control.py`, run over all 92 sessions / 552 position maps. Two independent
checks, and the pre-stroke epoch is the control for both.

**The decisive one is the isosbestic channel.** At 415 nm GCaMP does not report calcium, so that
series carries haemodynamics and noise and nothing else. Building the SAME position map from it --
same trials, same window, same basis, only the signal swapped -- and correlating against the
corrected map:

| epoch | sessions | maps | r(corrected, 415) | r(\|corrected\|, vessel) | r(\|415\|, vessel) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre | 44 | 264 | **-0.414** | -0.062 | -0.028 |
| acute | 16 | 96 | **-0.222** | -0.060 | -0.083 |
| subacute | 18 | 108 | -0.338 | -0.050 | -0.139 |
| chronic | 14 | 84 | -0.370 | -0.043 | -0.031 |

**Haemodynamic coupling does not rise after the stroke -- it FALLS**, from |r| = 0.41 pre to 0.22
acutely. Contamination growing post-stroke was the whole worry and it is the opposite of what
happened. The correlation is NEGATIVE throughout, which is the expected physiological sign rather
than a problem: neural activity raises blood volume, which LOWERS 415 fluorescence, so a real
calcium map should be anti-correlated with the isosbestic map of the same trials.

**Vessel alignment of the corrected map is small and flat across epochs** (-0.062 to -0.043) and
slightly NEGATIVE -- map amplitude sits marginally away from vessels, which is what dark, low-signal
pixels should do. It never converges on the 415 map's vessel correlation, which meanwhile wanders
(-0.028 -> -0.083 -> -0.139 -> -0.031). The two are not tracking each other.

**What this does NOT show.** A control can only fail to find contamination. Both checks are
correlations over whole maps and would miss an artefact confined to a small region. Read it as "not
large enough to see by two methods", never as "there is none".

## THE ONE THING TO READ FIRST

**Four of the six rows in figure 14 cannot be read as written**, and the reason is structural, not a
bug. Priya found it: *"in acute there may be less ss-ul/ll activity in far-center trials, which makes
the near ipsi acute trial map look as though there is a relative increase in ss-ul/ll activity."*

The Haufe pattern is `cov(pixel, decoder output)` on **trial-mean-centred** data, so the reference is
the average over all six positions. One position losing drive lowers the reference and hands every
other position an unearned increase. **The six maps are not independent.**

| withdrawn — cannot be read as increases | survives |
|---|---|
| near-middle 1.53 post-cue, 2.82 post-lick, 3.30 pre-cue | **far-contra 0.47** |
| far-ipsi 1.09 | **far-middle 0.48** |

The **falls** survive because the mechanism works against them: a position's own loss raises its
reference less than it lowers everyone else's. Read figure 14 as *"which positions fall"*, never
*"which positions change"*.

## Conclusions that stand

**The anatomical arm converges with two prior methods.** Far-contra and far-middle lose more than
half their map amplitude acutely and recover by subacute (0.83 / 0.91). The encoder's fitted
amplitude factor says the same in components rather than pixels: **0.749 pre → 0.286 acute → 0.745
chronic**. And the encoder ceiling's displaced-vs-degraded split names the same pair. Three methods
sharing almost no machinery.

**Low acute reliability IS the result, not a caveat against it** (Priya: *"the hanging map during
acute may be biological no?"* — yes, and I had it backwards). A split-half correlation of a
near-absent signal is low *because* the signal is near-absent. Far-contra acute is r = 0.53 **and**
0.47 amplitude — one observation. Far-middle falls to 0.48 amplitude while **keeping** r = 0.86,
which is what shows the two are separable.

**The pre-stroke basis is not a problem**, and not for the reason first given. Session SVD rank
**100**, joint basis **100** dims, overlap **0.996–0.997** — nearly a rotation, not a reduction. The
real truncation is the rank-100 SVD in *preprocessing*, upstream and identical pre/post, so it
cannot bias a pre-vs-post comparison.

## Design decisions, each measured

| decision | evidence |
|---|---|
| **SVT, not LocaNMF** features | SVT beats LocaNMF by 0.06–0.09 balanced accuracy; `U @ A` gives true pixel maps |
| **L2, not the paper's L1** | split-half of pre-stroke mean map: L1 0.688, L2 0.715, **L2+Haufe 0.960** |
| **Haufe transform** | single-session reliability **0.32 → 0.85**; pattern vs filter r = 0.245 |
| **class weights, not down-sampling** | down-sampling discarded 325 of 355 acute trials AND punished all six positions for one position's scarcity |
| **trial floor = 20, per position** | n=3 → 0.66–0.73; **n=5 → one map at r = −0.08**, anti-correlated with itself; n=20 → 0.81–0.96 |
| **delta shares the data's colour scale** | scaling a difference to its own range stretched noise across the full range |

**Read the pattern for where the signal is; the filter for what the decoder uses.** Different
questions. A pattern map is *not* evidence that those pixels are necessary for decoding.

## Bugs found and fixed today

1. **Every arm was fitted on LICKING trials only**, including those labelled `working` —
   `session_maps` discarded the no-lick arm. Surfaced because figure 14 refused the far-contra
   acute cell (0–9 trials vs 66–119 elsewhere). **Fixed**: far-contra acute 0 → 105 trials, while
   pre-stroke barely moves (87 → 91). *All amplitude numbers above were measured before this fix and
   need re-deriving.*
2. **`map_grid` crashed all three arms at exit code 0** — `np.concatenate([])` on a row with no
   delta cell. Fixed and pinned.
3. **`--only 14m` was rejected** — the key reached the dispatch and default set but not `choices`.
   Third instance today of "the figure exists and nothing can ask for it".
4. **Residual-decode control cannot work at rank 100** and gave a convincing wrong answer (0.93
   pre-stroke) before being caught. Kept in `basis_residual.py` as the record.
