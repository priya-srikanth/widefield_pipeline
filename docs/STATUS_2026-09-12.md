## THE ONE THING TO READ FIRST

**Four of the six rows in figure 14 cannot be read as written**, and the reason is structural, not a
bug. Priya found it: *"in acute there may be less ss-ul/ll activity in far-center trials, which makes
the near ipsi acute trial map look as though there is a relative increase in ss-ul/ll activity."*

The Haufe pattern is `cov(pixel, decoder output)` on **trial-mean-centred** data, so the reference is
the average over all six positions. One position losing drive lowers the reference and hands every
other position an unearned increase. **The six maps are not independent.**

| withdrawn — cannot be read as increases | survives |
|---|---|
| near-middle 1.53 post-cue, 2.82 post-lick, 3.30 pre-cue | **far-contra 0.47** |
| far-ipsi 1.09 | **far-middle 0.48** |

The **falls** survive because the mechanism works against them: a position's own loss raises its
reference less than it lowers everyone else's. Read figure 14 as *"which positions fall"*, never
*"which positions change"*.

## Conclusions that stand

**The anatomical arm converges with two prior methods.** Far-contra and far-middle lose more than
half their map amplitude acutely and recover by subacute (0.83 / 0.91). The encoder's fitted
amplitude factor says the same in components rather than pixels: **0.749 pre → 0.286 acute → 0.745
chronic**. And the encoder ceiling's displaced-vs-degraded split names the same pair. Three methods
sharing almost no machinery.

**Low acute reliability IS the result, not a caveat against it** (Priya: *"the hanging map during
acute may be biological no?"* — yes, and I had it backwards). A split-half correlation of a
near-absent signal is low *because* the signal is near-absent. Far-contra acute is r = 0.53 **and**
0.47 amplitude — one observation. Far-middle falls to 0.48 amplitude while **keeping** r = 0.86,
which is what shows the two are separable.

**The pre-stroke basis is not a problem**, and not for the reason first given. Session SVD rank
**100**, joint basis **100** dims, overlap **0.996–0.997** — nearly a rotation, not a reduction. The
real truncation is the rank-100 SVD in *preprocessing*, upstream and identical pre/post, so it
cannot bias a pre-vs-post comparison.

## Design decisions, each measured

| decision | evidence |
|---|---|
| **SVT, not LocaNMF** features | SVT beats LocaNMF by 0.06–0.09 balanced accuracy; `U @ A` gives true pixel maps |
| **L2, not the paper's L1** | split-half of pre-stroke mean map: L1 0.688, L2 0.715, **L2+Haufe 0.960** |
| **Haufe transform** | single-session reliability **0.32 → 0.85**; pattern vs filter r = 0.245 |
| **class weights, not down-sampling** | down-sampling discarded 325 of 355 acute trials AND punished all six positions for one position's scarcity |
| **trial floor = 20, per position** | n=3 → 0.66–0.73; **n=5 → one map at r = −0.08**, anti-correlated with itself; n=20 → 0.81–0.96 |
| **delta shares the data's colour scale** | scaling a difference to its own range stretched noise across the full range |

**Read the pattern for where the signal is; the filter for what the decoder uses.** Different
questions. A pattern map is *not* evidence that those pixels are necessary for decoding.

## Bugs found and fixed today

1. **Every arm was fitted on LICKING trials only**, including those labelled `working` —
   `session_maps` discarded the no-lick arm. Surfaced because figure 14 refused the far-contra
   acute cell (0–9 trials vs 66–119 elsewhere). **Fixed**: far-contra acute 0 → 105 trials, while
   pre-stroke barely moves (87 → 91). *All amplitude numbers above were measured before this fix and
   need re-deriving.*
2. **`map_grid` crashed all three arms at exit code 0** — `np.concatenate([])` on a row with no
   delta cell. Fixed and pinned.
3. **`--only 14m` was rejected** — the key reached the dispatch and default set but not `choices`.
   Third instance today of "the figure exists and nothing can ask for it".
4. **Residual-decode control cannot work at rank 100** and gave a convincing wrong answer (0.93
   pre-stroke) before being caught. Kept in `basis_residual.py` as the record.
