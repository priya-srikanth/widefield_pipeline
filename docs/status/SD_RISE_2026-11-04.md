# Post-stroke SD rise of the decoder features: gain or added noise? (2026-11-04)

Cohort 1 (PS92-95). Read-only diagnostic. No production module, model, render, deck or page was changed. Scripts, CSVs
and logs are in `M:/MICROSCOPE/Priya/Widefield/labcams/decoder_mix_checks/sd_rise_1104/`.

## 1. Question

After the stroke the per-session SD of the decoder features rises: trial rows ~1.1-1.4x, rest frames 1.2-1.5x
(REST_CENTRE_2026-11-04 §5d; INJECTED_EFFECT_2026-11-04 §7). Is that a GAIN change or ADDED NOISE?

- **Gain:** signal and noise scale together. This could be a measurement change (for example, a lower F0 inflating
  dF/F) or a global change in excitability or arousal.
- **Added noise:** within-spout variance rises while the spout signal stays the same.

Priya prefers per-session SD (Wt) for the frozen decoder. She also notes that the regional traces show larger lick
responses after the stroke.

### Check 1: variance split (`varsplit.py`, `summarise_varsplit.py`)

Computed per session, on the camera working rows, in raw dF/F, per feature:

- **noise** = pooled within-spout variance.
- **signal** = between-spout variance of the spout means, bias-corrected by −mean(noise / n).
- Both are expressed relative to the animal's pre, in pre-noise units, and reported as SD ratios.
- **snr** = signal / noise.
  - snr = 1: gain.
  - snr < 1: added noise.
- **gain** = the projection of the session's spout-mean pattern on the pre pattern. **r** = their correlation.

There are two spout sets:
- **ref** = `trial_classes.REFERENCE_SPOUTS` (close_L, close_center);
- **all** = the six spouts.

The `c_*` columns repeat the split on contact rows only, which removes the contact / incomplete mix.

### Check 2: F0 (`f0.py`, `summarise_f0_rest.py`)

**How the pipeline makes dF/F.** `run_wfield_local.py:106-149`:
- `approximate_svd(dat, frames_average)` divides each frame by its own channel's session-mean image;
- so F0 = `frames_average.npy` (raw counts, the 470 slot from `hemo_variants.functional_channel`), and every SVT /
  SVTcorr / decoder feature is ΔF / F0.

**What was measured, per session:**
- F0 470 and 415: the median over the eroded brain ROI (the `crossday_intensity` rule), and per Allen area
  (`frames_average_atlas`);
- the adopted hemo variant's `rcoeffs` (median) and `T` (Frobenius norm).

**Checks:**
- The central-60% F0 reproduces wf_attempt's `iti_f0_check.csv` exactly (106 / 106 sessions).
- No session in this set has a swap-repaired mean.
- Every `hemo_meegkit_hpfit` has `refit_T = false`, so its T / rcoeffs are the original fit.

### Check 3: task vs rest (`summarise_f0_rest.py`)

The quantities, per decoder column, as post / pre ratios (median over columns):

- **trial** = the working-row SD (the 4-bin box-mean feature).
- **rest** = the R5 box SD (restdock05, engaged) from rest_centre_1104 `rest/`, mapped onto the pool columns with its own
  keying.
- **R5q** = the same with frames −0.5 to +1.0 s around quiet-period orofacial events removed.
- **rest/trial** = (rest SD / trial SD) / pre.

### Check 4: lick responses (`lickresp.py`)

- with the ROI masks rebuilt from each session's atlas files: the column lists match in 107 / 107 sessions.

- **No.** F0 rose acutely; it did not fall.

- **Across sessions within an animal**, r(log SD ratio, log F0_pre/F0) is negative or zero:

## 7. Reading

### Gain, added noise, or both?

**Mostly a global gain, plus some added noise in PS93 acutely and a separate signal increase later.**

- **Acute, pooled: the within-spout noise and the between-spout signal rise together.**
  - All spouts: snr .95 [−.20, +.13]. Reference spouts: .94 [−.42, +.44].
  - Rest frames rise as much as trials or more.
  - That is the signature of a common scale change, not of trial-only noise.
- **It is not a dF/F measurement artefact.**
  - F0 rose (+9% acutely), which would shrink dF/F.
  - 1/F0 does not track the SD across sessions or across regions.
  - The haemodynamic coefficients do not move consistently.
  - A cortex-wide change in excitability or arousal fits. So would a change in haemodynamic or neurovascular coupling
    of the same size in task and rest; these data cannot separate the two.
- **Per animal it is not uniform:**
  - **PS94:** gain (snr .94-.96 acute, all / ref).
  - **PS93 acute:** ADDED NOISE (snr .75 all, .53 ref; noise 1.16-1.28 while the signal falls).
  - **PS92 acute:** gain at all spouts (1.13), but the reference-spout signal falls (ref snr .66).
  - **PS95:** one acute session, signal up more than noise.
- **Not a pure gain even where the sizes match.**
  - The reference-spout pattern (close_L vs close_center) does not keep its shape: pattern r falls from .72 to .33
    acutely (CI below 0) and to .22 chronically.
  - In PS93 it reverses (−.21 acute, −.60 chronic).
  - So the close-spout signal changes direction, not only size.
- **Chronic: the between-spout signal rises more than the noise** (all snr 1.32 [−.06, +.68]; 3 / 4 animals > 1.3,
  PS94 .78).
  - This is a signal increase on top of the global scale.
  - It matches the later lick-response result.

### Does per-session SD (Wt) look right for the frozen decoder?

**Yes, as the primary.**

- Because the rise is shared by noise, signal and rest, dividing it out per session is what removes a global scale
  without touching the spout pattern.
- A fixed scale (WtF) would carry the scale into every margin. That is consistent with WtF's +.10 acute far-contra
  inflation (INJECTED_EFFECT §7d), which is carried by PS94 / PS95, the two animals with the largest gain-like rises.
- No scale frame can undo what this check also shows:
  - PS93's acute added noise (an SNR loss of ~25-45%);
  - the change in the shape of the reference-spout pattern.
- Those are real changes in the features that any frame will show.
- The chronic signal increase is partly divided out by Wt. Reporting WtF beside Wt as the "gain-sensitive" reading
  (§7e there) stays appropriate.

### Does the lick-response increase depend on dF/F normalisation?

- **Not on F0.** Normalising by F0 makes it larger.
- **On per-session z-scoring, partly:**
  - the ACUTE cued-lick increase is entirely the session's global scale-up and does not survive (c) or (c');
  - the subacute / chronic cued increase in contralesional tjM1 / SSp-m survives at about a third of its size (4 / 4

  - the docked-ITI increase survives in contralesional SSp-m / tjM1 at about 60% of its size.
- **The slide's acute cued claim (PS94 / PS95)** is the same gain that raises the decoder-feature SD.
- **The later and the ITI increases** are larger than that gain.

## 8. Limits

- **n = 4 animals.** PS95 has ONE acute session and PS92 TWO subacute sessions. The PS92 subacute values (2.1-3.5x)
  dominate the subacute pooled cells.
- **Reference-spout signal is two spouts.** Its unbiased variance is noisy per session (pooled CIs ±.4). The six-spout
  split is tighter, but it includes the stroke's own spout-specific change (far contra), which pushes the signal down
  and so works against a gain reading.
- **Rest SD is a box-mean approximation** of the trial feature, as in REST_CENTRE §7.
- **F0 per ROI uses `frames_average_atlas`.** That is the camera-grid mean warped to the atlas, the same image the
  pipeline divides by. LED power is titrated by hand per day; that cancels in dF/F but not in raw F0, so the F0 ratios
  partly reflect the LED setting. This does not affect the conclusion, which rests on the sign: F0 did not fall.
- **The z-score SDs in check 4 come from the lick windows.**
  - (c) includes the response itself, so it removes any change that scales the whole window. That is the intent here,
    but it is conservative for a response that grows faster than the background.
  - (c') uses only the pre-lick part. For cued licks that part can include the cue in slow-RT trials.
