## T20 — synchronization index (SI = log10 PSD 1-4 Hz / 8-15 Hz)

**Question.** Is cortex more synchronized after the stroke (reduced tonic thalamocortical drive)? Is the effect largest in
orofacial cortex, and ipsilesional if it goes through the VLS loop?

**Method.**
- QUIET: the existing quiet-engaged spectra (`v2_quiet_lp/fluct`; Welch 4-s segments on quiet runs >= 4 s; no LP / DAQ lick
  or running within 1.5 s; no swapped pair within 0.5 s).
- PRE-CUE (new): strobe to cue of engaged trials, with no lick (LP + DAQ) or running bout within 0.5 s and no swapped pair
  (the `quiet_task_events` windows). Welch 2-s segments on clean runs >= 2 s.
- Traces are `quiet_area_spikes.session_traces` (adopted hemo-corrected SVD, dF/F %).
- 108 sessions (pre 32, acute 16, subacute 17, chronic 43).

**Per animal, change vs pre (log10).**

| animal | quiet oro bilat a / s / c | pre-cue oro bilat a / s / c | quiet oro ipsi-contra acute | pre-cue oro ipsi-contra acute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS92 | +0.52 / +0.29 / +0.04 | +0.31 / +0.29 / +0.27 | +0.07 | +0.02 |
| PS93 | +0.82 / +0.13 / +0.12 | +0.85 / +0.17 / +0.18 | +0.23 | +0.28 |
| PS94 | +0.77 / +0.52 / -0.02 | +0.87 / +0.37 / +0.17 | +0.11 | +0.12 |
| PS95 (1 acute session) | +0.57 / +0.23 / +0.04 | +0.41 / +0.19 / +0.04 | +0.29 | +0.24 |

**Pooled (acute).**
- Orofacial bilateral, quiet: +0.67 [0.47, 0.88], LMM p .007, 4/4. Pre-cue: +0.61 [0.34, 0.89], p .026, 4/4.
- Orofacial minus limb, quiet: +0.53 [0.31, 0.79], p .014, 4/4. Pre-cue: +0.56 [0.46, 0.69], p .005, 4/4.
- Orofacial minus cortex, quiet: +0.29, p .17, 4/4. Pre-cue: +0.27, p .016, 4/4.
- Limb bilateral: +0.14 quiet, +0.05 pre-cue (2/4).
- Subacute is about half the acute size. Chronic reverses: cortex and limb SI rise, and orofacial minus cortex is -0.29,
  4/4.
- Laterality, orofacial ipsi - contra: +0.17 [0.06, 0.29] quiet (p .066) and +0.16 [0.05, 0.27] pre-cue (p .041), 4/4 in
  both. Limb ipsi - contra shows the same shift: +0.19 quiet, +0.16 pre-cue. Orofacial laterality minus limb laterality is
  about 0 (-0.01 quiet, 1/4; -0.00 pre-cue, 2/4).
- None of the 48 cells passes Holm.

**Which band moves (Fig T20-S1).**
- The orofacial bilateral SI rise comes from 1-4 Hz power: +0.64 quiet, +0.54 pre-cue, 4/4. 8-15 Hz power is unchanged
  (-0.03 / -0.06).
- The ipsi > contra part comes from the DENOMINATOR. 8-15 Hz power is lower ipsilesionally: orofacial -0.09 / -0.10
  (4/4), limb -0.14 / -0.15 (4/4). Limb quiet is the only Holm-surviving cell, p .0005, Holm .025.
- 1-4 Hz ipsi - contra is n.s. (+0.09 / +0.06).

**Per ROI (Fig T20-S2).**
- Bilateral SI rises acutely in every orofacial ROI and both frame types (ALM +0.51 / +0.59, tjM1 +0.58 / +0.67, SSp-m
  +0.52 / +0.55, SSp-n +0.38 / +0.39; all 4/4).
- Ipsi - contra is positive mostly in SSp-m and SSp-n (+0.21 to +0.24, 3-4/4), not in ALM (+0.10 to +0.11) or tjM1.

**Verdict vs prediction.**
- "More synchronized acutely, strongest in orofacial": **yes, descriptively.** It holds 4/4 in quiet and in pre-cue
  frames, and it is a 1-4 Hz rise, not a fast-power loss. It is not Holm-significant at n = 4.
- "Ipsilesional": **not supported.** The ipsi > contra SI comes from lower 8-15 Hz power on the lesioned side, which limb
  shows as strongly. This matches the 11-01 withdrawal (T22 / T20b: a non-calcium 7-11 Hz peak).

**Caveats.**
- At 31 Hz with GCaMP6s, 8-15 Hz is near the noise floor. That is why the index's laterality is not interpretable.
- Pre-cue uses 2-s Welch segments (0.5 Hz bins) and quiet uses 4-s, so absolute pre levels differ. Changes vs pre are
  comparable, and a 2-s quiet check gives the same answer (Fig T20-S2).
- PS95 has 1 acute session.
- The acute contrast shares sessions with the k-choice of the event detector. The SI does not use k.

## T20b — SI with a 4-8 Hz denominator + 415-nm control

**Question.** Does the laterality survive a fast band that avoids the 7-11 Hz non-calcium peak?

**Method.** SI48 = log10(P1-4 / P4-8), quiet and pre-cue. Control: 415-nm spectra on the same quiet frames
(`v2_quiet_lp/hemo415`).

**Results (acute).**
- Orofacial bilateral SI48: +0.64 quiet (p .008, 4/4), +0.45 pre-cue (p .015, 4/4).
- Orofacial ipsi - contra: +0.10 quiet (p .19, 3/4), +0.04 pre-cue (p .38, 2/4).
- Orofacial laterality minus limb laterality: -0.02 / -0.03.
- The **415-nm** channel has its own lesion-side SI asymmetry, orofacial +0.11 (p .018, 4/4) and limb +0.08 (4/4), with
  415 4-8 Hz power lower ipsilesionally (-0.06, 4/4).
- Per-animal tables: `t20b_SI48_stats_per_animal.txt`.

**Verdict.** The bilateral orofacial synchronization stands with the cleaner band. The laterality shrinks to about 0.04-0.10,
is not orofacial-specific, and is matched by the hemodynamic channel. **The 11-02 conclusion (withdrawal stands) holds.**

**Caveat.** The top of the 4-8 Hz band still touches the 7-11 Hz peak, and 415 shows that 4-8 Hz is not artifact-free.

## T21 — within PRE sessions, does pre-lick slow power predict the lick response?

**Question.** Licks that start from a high 1-4 Hz (synchronized) state: do they evoke larger responses? If so, a state
effect could explain part of the acute ~2x lick response.

**Method.**
- Data: the T21 per-lick table (LP onsets + unmatched DAQ contacts, engaged). Licks are excluded if another lick falls in
  -1..0 s, running overlaps, or there is a swap. 32 August-pre sessions (8 per animal).
- Pre-lick power: 1-4 Hz over -1.0..-0.1 s (the window -0.1..0 is excluded to avoid onset leak).
- Response: mean 0..0.5 s minus mean -0.5..-0.1 s.
- Per session: OLS of response on z(power), in three variants:
  - A: Hilbert power, zero-phase filtered (as specified, acausal).
  - B: causal power (forward filter; leak control).
  - C: B plus the baseline level as a covariate (the baseline window sits inside the power window).
- Per animal: mean of the session slopes, plus a lick-level pooled within-session test.
- Pooled: `boot_ci` (nested) and a t test of the 4 animal means. Holm across 24 cells (2 lick kinds x 4 columns x 3
  variants).

**Per animal (cued first lick, orofacial bilateral, % dF/F per SD).**

| animal | A Hilbert (p lick-level) | B causal (p) | C causal + baseline (p) | B as % of mean response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS92 | 0.39 (<1e-4) | 0.14 (1e-4) | 0.12 (<1e-4) | 4.3 % |
| PS93 | 0.28 (<1e-4) | 0.12 (<1e-4) | 0.06 (.005) | 4.6 % |
| PS94 | 0.15 (<1e-4) | 0.00 (.81) | -0.01 (.18) | 0.2 % |
| PS95 | 0.18 (<1e-4) | 0.03 (.06) | 0.02 (.16) | 1.2 % |

ITI bout-initiating licks, B causal: 0.17 / 0.14 / 0.07 / 0.09 (PS92-95; p < 1e-4 / < 1e-4 / .012 / .26). That is 5-14 % of
a smaller mean response.

**Pooled.**
- Cued, orofacial: A +0.25 [0.16, 0.37] (t p .020, 4/4 significant individually). B +0.07 [0.01, 0.15] (p .11; 2/4
  significant). C +0.05 [-0.00, 0.10] (p .19; 2/4).
- ITI, orofacial: B +0.12 [0.06, 0.17] (p .014; 3/4 significant).
- Ipsi and contra orofacial are similar. Whole cortex, B: about 0 (-0.02 cued, +0.01 ITI).
- None of the 24 cells passes Holm. Only the acausal A variant is consistent in 4/4 animals individually.

**Verdict vs prediction ("yes -> state explains part").**
- A weak yes: licks from higher slow-power states give slightly larger orofacial responses.
- Most of the "as specified" effect is filter leak. The causal slope is about 1/3 of the Hilbert slope.
- With causal power and a baseline covariate, the slope is about 2-5 % of the response per SD, and is reliable only in
  PS92 / PS93.
- So pre-stroke state explains little of the response size. The acute 1-4 Hz rise (about +0.6 log10, x4 power) is too
  small a lever to explain a ~2x response. This agrees with the 11-02 reading (upper bound 14-22 %).

**Caveats.**
- This is a within-session correlation, not a manipulation.
- The power and baseline windows overlap (handled by variant C).
- The lick-free check before the lick uses DAQ contacts, as in the original script.
- There is no limb column in the cached table; the whole cortex is the control.

## T22 — per-area F0 beyond the 415-predicted part

**Question.** Is there a tonic level drop in ipsilesional orofacial cortex (reduced drive)?

**Method.**
- F0 = whole-session mean image per Allen ROI, both channels, from the T22 table (`v2_quiet/f0_noise/f0_sessions.csv`).
  The 470 slot is `hemo_variants.functional_channel` (0 for PS92_0828). F0 does not depend on the quiet mask.
- F0res = log10 F470 - (a_ROI + b · log10 F415), with b = each animal's within-ROI slope across August-pre sessions
  (b = 0.82 / 0.96 / 0.12 / 0.58; r = .65 / .94 / .12 / .73).
- Sensitivity checks: log10 F470/F415 (b = 1), raw log10 F470, raw log10 F415.

**Per animal, F0res change (log10).**

| PS92 | +0.069 / +0.082 / +0.009 | +0.057 / +0.065 / +0.008 | +0.052 / +0.027 / -0.004 | -0.021 / -0.019 / 0.000 |
| PS93 | +0.040 / +0.148 / -0.021 | +0.036 / +0.157 / -0.020 | +0.006 / +0.017 / +0.003 | +0.010 / -0.001 / +0.008 |
| PS94 | +0.063 / +0.036 / -0.043 | +0.038 / +0.017 / -0.013 | +0.004 / -0.005 / -0.003 | -0.012 / -0.005 / 0.000 |
| PS95 | -0.009 / +0.015 / -0.030 | +0.007 / +0.002 / -0.019 | -0.001 / +0.006 / 0.000 | +0.002 / -0.007 / +0.001 |

**Pooled.**
- Orofacial bilateral: acute +0.041 [0.002, 0.081] (p .043, 3/4) and subacute +0.070 (p .08, 4/4), i.e. about 10-17 %
  higher. Limb rises equally (+0.035 / +0.060).
- Orofacial ipsi - contra: acute +0.016 [0.001, 0.040] (p .29) — ipsi slightly HIGHER, carried by PS92. Subacute +0.011,
  chronic -0.001.
- Orofacial minus limb laterality: -0.005 / -0.008 / +0.002. Per ROI, ALM, tjM1, SSp-m and SSp-n ipsi - contra are all
  >= 0 acutely.
- No cell passes Holm (27).
- The sensitivity measures give the same picture (ratio: orofacial ipsi - contra +0.013; raw 470 +0.029).

**Verdict vs prediction ("relative ipsi orofacial F0 drop").** **Not supported.** No animal shows an ipsilesional
orofacial F0 drop. F0 rises bilaterally and hemisphere-wide (orofacial = limb) acutely and subacutely, then returns or dips
slightly chronically.

**Caveats.** F0 is a WEAK tonic proxy:
- It mixes GCaMP expression, blood volume and oxygenation, light path / focus, bleaching and window clarity.
- The 415 correction is a linear fit across pre sessions, and it is poor in PS94 (r .12).
- A tonic drop of a few percent would be invisible next to the 10-17 % global F0 rise.
- Absence of evidence here is not evidence against reduced drive.

## Bottom line for Priya's reduced-drive hypothesis

1. Acutely, orofacial cortex is more synchronized in both quiet and pre-cue frames, 4/4 animals. This is a 1-4 Hz rise
   relative to limb and cortex.
2. There is no lesion-side signature: not in synchronization (it is an 8-15 Hz / 415 artifact, shared with limb) and not in
   F0.
3. Pre-stroke state predicts only a small, mostly leak-driven part of lick-response size.

None of this passes Holm at n = 4. Conclusions rest on sign consistency, and they are unchanged from 11-01 / 11-02.
