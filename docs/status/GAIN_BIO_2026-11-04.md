# Is the post-stroke SD rise a biological gain or a measurement change? (2026-11-04)

Cohort 1 (PS92-95). Read-only diagnostic. No production module, model, render, deck or page was changed. Scripts, CSVs,
figures and logs are in `M:/MICROSCOPE/Priya/Widefield/labcams/decoder_mix_checks/gain_bio_1104/`. Follows
SD_RISE_2026-11-04.md.

## 1. Question

After the stroke the SD of activity rises in task and at rest (SD_RISE: about 1.2x acutely, a shared gain). F0 rose and
the haemodynamic coefficients did not shift. Per-session z-scoring removes the gain, and with it the acute "lick
response larger" result. Is the gain a real biological change?

1. **Spatial map.** Is the gain uniform across cortex (more likely global: optical, measurement or brain-wide state), or
   concentrated in orofacial / anterolateral or ipsilesional cortex (more likely local biology)? Does the 415 nm channel
   show it?
2. **Movement and arousal at rest.** Does the gain survive matching or regressing out face movement and running, and far
   from quiet events?

## 2. Design (`gain_bio.py`, `summarise_gain_bio.py`)

**Sessions:** the 107 SD_RISE sessions (31 August pre). Each value is the session's value divided by the mean of the
animal's pre sessions; a pre session is compared with the animal's OTHER pre sessions, so the pre rows read ~1.00 and
serve as the null. Per animal x epoch = geometric mean over sessions. The pooled change is `analysis_kit.boot_delta`
(post − held-out pre, animals → sessions, 95%); k/4 = animals whose mean rose.

**Signals.** All are dF/F on the same U_atlas basis:

| name | what |
|---|---|
| corr | production hemo-corrected 470 (`hemo_meegkit_hpfit` SVTcorr), what the decoder and lick slides use |
| corr_hp | corr with a 0.1 Hz high-pass (Butterworth order 2, zero phase) |
| r470_hp | uncorrected 470 (the functional half of raw SVT.npy), same high-pass |
| r415_hp | 415 nm isosbestic half of raw SVT.npy, same high-pass: the non-calcium control |

The slot comes from `hemo_variants.functional_channel` and is checked against the hemo manifest (the quiet_hemo_check
loader logic).

**Areas:** `scripts/iti_licks.roi_masks` (the quiet-events arm's masks). These are every Allen area per hemisphere on the
eroded stat mask, plus ALM / tjM1 (the anterolateral 40% of MOs / MOp) and cortex. `_left` = ipsilesional (image left).

**Pixel maps:** the per-pixel variance diag(U C Uᵀ), where C is the component covariance over the frame set, binned
4 x 4 px on the atlas grid.

**Frame sets.** Frames within 3 of a swapped pair are dropped from every set.

| set | frames |
|---|---|
| R5 | restdock05 quiet frames AND engaged (the rest_centre_1104 R5 rule) |
| R5far | R5 AND more than 2 s from any quiet event. Events: v2 detector, orofacial and limb L / R, both polarities, above 4x the animal's pre robust SD |
| TASK | cue to cue + 2 s, every cue before the quit |
| ALL | every frame |

**Movement and arousal proxies:**

- **Face speed:** cam4 FaceRhythm point-PC speed (`quiet_spikes_fr.face_regressors`, own basis), smoothed over 2 s.
- **Running:** treadmill speed (`behavior_events.session_speed`), smoothed over 2 s.
- **Pupil:** none exists for cohort 1 (no eye tracking; see the `quiet_quit_state.py` docstring).

**Rest chunks.** R5 is cut into chunks of at most 2 s inside contiguous runs. Each chunk stores its sums and sums of
squares, so the variance of any speed-matched subset can be rebuilt exactly. From the chunks:

- **Speed bins:** face speed / the animal's pre median: < .25, .25-.5, .5-1, 1-2, > 2.
- **matched:** the bin variances reweighted to the animal's pre speed distribution.
- **still:** face speed < 0.5x the pre median.
- **still_far:** still AND far from events.

**Face regression.** An out-of-fold ridge regression (10 contiguous folds) of each R5 area trace (corr_hp) on:

- the FaceRhythm design (factors, 20 point PCs and PC speeds; 2-s high-passed; lags −0.3, 0 and +0.3 s);
- plus smoothed face and treadmill speed.

The residual SD is then compared with the raw SD.

**Spatial correlations, per session, across areas:**

- log SD ratio vs log F0 ratio (`frames_average_atlas`; also across pixel bins);
- log R5 SD ratio vs log quiet-event rate ratio, over the 12 event areas (ALM, tjM1, SSp-m, SSp-n, SSp-ul, SSp-ll x
  L / R; `quiet_events_sessions.csv`, rate_both).

## 5. Reading

### Is the gain uniform or regional?

**Global, not regional.** The calcium gain covers the whole dorsal cortex in every animal and epoch:

- the area ratios are 1.2-1.5 acutely in all 46 areas, 4 / 4 animals in nearly all of them;
- spread across areas grows only slightly (+.03 log SD);
- there is no ipsi / contra difference (+.01 acutely; slightly contra > ipsi later);
- orofacial / anterolateral cortex is NOT preferentially affected (+.05 n.s. acutely);
- after the acute epoch the orofacial cortex rises LESS than posterior / medial cortex (RSP, VIS, ACAd): −.14 to −.21,
  0 / 4 animals the other way.

The peri-lesional projection field (ipsilesional ALM / tjM1 / SSp-m) shows no extra gain.

### Is it present in 415?

**Partly, about half the size, and with a different map.**

- 415 SD rises +.18 acutely (corr +.35), 4 / 4.
- That 415 rise is regional: ipsilesional and lateral (SSp-n / SSs / SSp-m left 1.6-1.7x vs right 1.35-1.4x; ipsi −
  contra +.07*, orofacial − posterior +.17*), plus a vessel pattern on the maps.
- The 415 rise tracks the 415 F0 change across areas (r +.46*).
- That reads as a peri-lesional haemodynamic / optical change in the 415 channel. It is NOT the shape of the calcium gain.
- The corrected 470 rises as much as the uncorrected 470 (1.19-1.64 vs 1.22-1.49 acutely), so the hemo correction is not
  injecting the gain, and the part of 470 that 415 does not predict rises.
- 415/corr_hp is < 1 in 10 of 12 post cells.
- Chronically the 415 rise is not reliable (+.18 [−.06, +.39]; PS94 0.86) while the calcium gain persists.

### Is it explained by arousal or movement?

**No, as far as the available proxies can measure.**

- The animals are STILLER acutely, yet the gain is as large at matched face speed (+.40), in the stillest frames (+.43),
  in every speed bin, and after regressing out face movement (+.42).
- Running is absent from rest frames.
- Session-level face speed does not explain the between-session gain.
- Away from quiet events the gain is about 80% of its size (+.28 still & far vs +.35). PS95 acute is the exception: its
  gain is mostly carried by event periods (far 1.04).
- Acutely the gain map co-varies with the quiet-event rate map (r +.27 above the pre null). That link goes away later.
- No pupil or other arousal measure exists. A hidden brain-state change that leaves the face still cannot be excluded.

### So: biological or measurement?

**More consistent with a biological, cortex-wide gain than with measurement:**

- the calcium channel rises beyond what 415 / haemodynamics predict;
- F0 rose (which deflates dF/F) and does not co-vary with the gain map acutely;
- the gain persists in matched still periods and away from events, in task and rest.

**But it is GLOBAL and not lesion-local.** It is no larger in the projection field and no larger ipsilesionally. That
fits a brain-wide change in excitability or state (for example, altered neuromodulatory / thalamic tone, or an
inflammatory state) better than a local circuit change.

**Implication for the acute "lick response larger" result.** Its acute part has the same size and the same
non-specificity as this global gain. Per-session z-scoring removes a real but cortex-wide change, not a pure artefact.
Whether to report the raw response (biological gain included) or the z-scored response (lick-specific beyond the global
gain) is a framing choice for Priya. These data say the global part is probably real, but it is not specific to licking
or to orofacial cortex.

## 6. Limits

- **n = 4 animals.** PS95 has ONE acute session and PS92 TWO subacute sessions. The PS92 subacute corr values (2.1x) are
  inflated by slow drift: corr_hp gives 1.36.
- **415 is not a pure null.** Haemodynamic fluctuations are neurally driven, so a real neural gain would also raise 415
  variance somewhat. A 415 rise of about half the calcium rise is therefore ambiguous on its own. The discriminating
  evidence is its different, ipsilateral / vascular map and the corrected-470 rise.
- **Arousal proxies are face speed (FaceRhythm) and running only.** There is no pupil, EEG or heart rate. "Not explained
  by movement" does not mean "not a state change".
- **Quiet-event rates use a fixed pre-derived threshold**, so a gain inflates them mechanically. The positive acute
  correlation with the event-rate map is partly circular (the pre null is already r ~.4-.7).
- **The high-pass is a 0.1 Hz Butterworth** for the raw channels, not production's meegkit detrend. corr and corr_hp
  agree (except PS92 subacute).
- **TASK = every engaged cue, 0-2 s**, not the decoder's camera working rows.
- **ROI masks are this repo's `scripts/iti_licks` (v1, 40%)**, as in the quiet-events arm. The lick slide used
  wf_attempt's v2 masks.
- **Pixel maps are on the shared atlas grid**, so registration jitter is in the edges. The midline / sinus and the rim
  are noisy in the 415 maps.
