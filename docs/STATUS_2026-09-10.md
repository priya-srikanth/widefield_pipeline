## Three structural changes, each of which moves published numbers

### 1. Epoch boundaries moved — chronic is now THREE animals

The chronic FLAT test was a per-session slope compared against a tolerance calibrated on pre-stroke
session-to-session scatter. Post-stroke tail sessions sit **3.3–3.7x further apart** than the
typical pre-stroke session (median gap 1 day), so the test was ~3.5x **stricter** exactly where
plateaus get declared. The note in `epochs.py` claimed the opposite bias; it was wrong and is
corrected.

Fixed by testing **total drift across the window** (`flat_mode: drift`), which is invariant to how
many sessions a window holds and how far apart they are. Per-day fitting was tried and overcorrects
— it passes PS95 from day 2 on a series still climbing 83 to 104%.

The **level bars** ("recovered to >= 90% / >= 80% of baseline") are retired. Priya: "I have had
prior stroke animals that plateau well below pre-stroke baseline … the plateau (defined AFTER the
acute to subacute transition) should be enough." A level bar asserts where an animal must END UP;
chronic is a claim about it having STOPPED CHANGING. The flat-at-zero hole the bars guarded is
closed instead by a candidate floor at each animal's own `subacute_from`.

| animal | chronic from | why |
|---|---|---|
| PS92 | 11 | unchanged, qualifies under both flat tests |
| PS93 | **11** | was blocked by licks alone, by 0.001 of tolerance |
| PS94 | none | licks now plateau at day 9 (~50% of baseline) and COUNT; the HIT rate is still moving — day 22 to 25 jumps 0.732 to 1.000, which is 0.208 of drift against a 0.061 tolerance |
| PS95 | **15** | moved LATER from 11, which the behaviour box promoted under the old rule before PS95 had a day-25 session |

Published to `epoch_boundaries.json` and promoted into `animals.yaml`. **Every subacute and chronic
number in the prelim document is now stale**; acute is untouched.

### 2. Two measurement errors found, both inflating a headline

* **Best-match baseline.** One-hotted from the AVERAGED leave-one-session-out matrix while every
  post-stroke epoch averages PER-SESSION one-hots. `argmax(mean)` is not `mean(argmax)`, and the
  averaged form is a perfect identity for every animal. Far-contra's fall is **0.92, not 0.94**.
* **Encoder "half amplitude, half shape".** Withdrawn. EV after rescale is 0.134 acutely, and
  `_enc_terms` states the rule in its own docstring: rescaling recovering a lot means amplitude only
  when that number is HIGH, because a code that is simply gone also recovers a lot. The acute
  encoder failure is dominated by TUNING.

  Amplitude FELL (`a` 0.943 to 0.361), which is also what makes whole rows of the raw crossnobis
  epoch-minus-pre matrix red: `d = (1-a)^2 |mu|^2`, so a shrinking response is pushed away from the
  template exactly as a growing one would be. Largest in the pre-cue and lick arms at far-middle and
  far-contra.

### 3. One palette, one set of day ticks

Three position palettes coexisted, two of them in the same deck — hue-by-side (behaviour),
hue-by-RING (`grant_figures`, whose comment claimed it matched the behaviour figures), and six
unrelated tab10 colours (`locanmf_dff_by_position`, where far_R was purple). All now resolve through
`spout_behavior.position_style`: **hue = side (ipsi blue, middle purple, contra red), lightness =
ring (far paler)**.

Day ticks were filtered by PARITY (`d % 2 == 1`), so on a 1 2 3 4 5 7 9 11 15 18 22 25 schedule the
axis appeared to stop at day 15 while three more sessions were plotted past it. Now thinned by
index, with the last day always kept.

## New analyses built today

| family | what it answers |
|---|---|
| **5r** frozen-vs-refit | Is the code LOST or MISREAD? Refit within session on the SAME trials, paired at the trial level |
| **5rm** training-set matched | The same with both arms given equal training data |
| **10c** best-match destination | WHERE each position's code went — one other position (substitution) or spread evenly (collapse) |
| **8rz** row-normalised crossnobis | Direction only, scale-free; companion to 8rc |

**What 5r showed.** The no-lesion baseline is **bracketed, not zero**: unmatched, the frozen arm has
10x the training data (gap -0.073 post-cue); matched, it must generalise across sessions (gap
**+0.090**). Both are real effects and neither is "the" answer. The headline survives either way —
far-contra carries the largest lesion-attributable recoverable component, **+0.196 unmatched and
+0.158 matched** — but it is a MINORITY of the deficit: about a third of far-contra's acute drop is
recoverable, while far-ipsi and far-middle recover only 9% and 11%. "Relocated rather than lost" is
now "partly relocated, mostly lost, and the relocated part is position-specific".

**What 10c showed.** Acute far-contra keeps 0.06 of its sessions and sends **0.50 to far-middle**
and 0.25 to far-ipsi. Concentrated substitution, not dissolution.

**What the split-half off-diagonal showed** (Priya's reading, confirmed): each position stays about
as repeatable as before (diagonal 0.791 to 0.724) while the positions stop being distinguishable
from each other (off-diagonal -0.169 to **+0.091**). The merging is four times the size of the
reliability loss. Far-contra against far-ipsi goes -0.24 to **+0.58**, against far-middle +0.15 to
**+0.63**.

**A correction to my own claim.** I said the +0.260 vs +0.082 difference between split-half and
row-centred crossnobis meant "two thirds common shift, one third substitution". Wrong — those are a
correlation change and a normalised distance change: different units, no ratio to take. Split-half
is Pearson and is already fully gain-blind, so it has no amplitude component to remove.

And **row-centred crossnobis is not fully gain-blind either**. Writing the row out, the
position-dependent term scales linearly with that position's response amplitude while the remaining
term does not depend on the position at all. So row-centring removes gain from the row's OFFSET and
leaves it multiplying the row's SHAPE: direction and rank are comparable across epochs, magnitude is
not. Checked that the rows do not degenerate to a common profile when amplitude collapses
(between-row r = +0.02 pre, +0.23 acute, +0.01 subacute, +0.02 chronic), so the direction signal is
real at every epoch.
