## THE HEADLINE FINDING: displaced vs degraded, and they are different positions

Two analyses built today, opposite directions of fit, entirely different statistics, one dissociation:

> **Far-contralateral's code is DISPLACED. Its neighbours' codes are DEGRADED.**

| evidence | far-contra | far-ipsi / far-middle |
|---|---|---|
| encoder SHAPE ceiling, acute Δ | **−0.002** (0.628 → 0.626) | **−0.323 / −0.270** |
| encoder template capture, acute | 0.55 → **0.01** | 0.56 → 0.22 / 0.63 → 0.42 |
| decoder deficit recovered by refitting | **34%** | **9% / 11%** |
| best-match destination, acute | keeps 0.06, sends **0.50 → far-middle**, 0.25 → far-ipsi | — |

Far-contra's own trials predict each other exactly as well as before the lesion; what it loses is the
match to the pre-stroke template. Its neighbours lose the structure itself. **The pooled "~28% of
available structure is lost" averages those two opposite things and must not be quoted without the
split** — and "partly relocated, mostly lost" is wrong at the impaired position specifically, where
nothing measurable is lost.

## Structural changes, each of which moves published numbers

### 1. Epoch boundaries moved — chronic is now THREE animals

The chronic FLAT test was a per-session slope against a tolerance calibrated on pre-stroke
session-to-session scatter. Post-stroke tail sessions sit **3.3–3.7x further apart** than the typical
pre-stroke session (median gap 1 day), so the test was ~3.5x **stricter** exactly where plateaus get
declared. The note in `epochs.py` claimed the opposite bias and was corrected.

Now `flat_mode: drift` — total fitted change across the window, invariant to how many sessions a
window holds and how far apart they are. Per-day fitting was tried and overcorrects (PS95 passes from
day 2 on a series still climbing 83 → 104%).

**The level bars are retired.** Priya: "I have had prior stroke animals that plateau well below
pre-stroke baseline … the plateau (defined AFTER the acute to subacute transition) should be enough."
A level bar asserts where an animal must END UP; chronic is a claim about it having STOPPED CHANGING.
The flat-at-zero hole they guarded is closed by a candidate floor at each animal's own
`subacute_from` instead.

| animal | chronic from | why |
|---|---|---|
| PS92 | 11 | unchanged |
| PS93 | **11** | was blocked by licks alone, by 0.001 of tolerance |
| PS94 | none | licks now plateau at day 9 (~50% of baseline) and COUNT; the HIT rate is still moving — day 22→25 jumps 0.732→1.000 |
| PS95 | **15** | moved LATER from 11, which the behaviour box had promoted under the old rule before PS95 had a day-25 session |

Published to `epoch_boundaries.json`, promoted into `animals.yaml`. **Every subacute and chronic
number in the prelim document predates this**; acute is untouched.

### 2. Three measurement errors found, all inflating a headline

* **Best-match baseline** one-hotted from the AVERAGED leave-one-session-out matrix while every
  post-stroke epoch averages PER-SESSION one-hots. `argmax(mean) ≠ mean(argmax)`, and the averaged
  form is a perfect identity for every animal. Far-contra's fall is **0.92, not 0.94**.
* **Encoder "half amplitude, half shape"** — withdrawn, then replaced. See below.
* **Captured fraction had no distribution** — it divided one pooled number by another, so it had no
  interval and no dots. Now formed per session and pooled through the standard bootstrap.

### 3. Negative explained variance is an AMPLITUDE statement

`EV = 1 − SSE/SST` after centring across positions, so negative means the template is further from
the data than predicting zero — than assuming no position tuning at all. With PERFECT shape and only
a scale mismatch, `R² = 1 − (1−a)²/a²`: exactly **0.000 at a = 0.5** and **−2.13 at the a = 0.361
observed acutely**.

That confounded the first version of the ceiling comparison, since the ceiling's halves have matched
amplitude by construction while the frozen arm's reference does not. All arms are now scored after
rescale. **It changed the conclusion:** amplitude recovers (a 0.286 → 0.527 → **0.745** against 0.749
pre-stroke); shape does not (+0.192 → +0.128 → +0.186, flat). Numbers re-pulled at
`ENC_CEILING_REPEATS = 32` on 2026-09-10 by `scripts/prelim_numbers_encoder_ceiling.py`; the earlier
versions of this table were pulled at 8 and differ in the third decimal only.

### 4. One palette, one set of day ticks, one named engagement axis

* Three position palettes coexisted, two in the same deck. All now resolve through
  `spout_behavior.position_style`: **hue = side (ipsi blue, middle purple, contra red), lightness =
  ring (far paler)**.
* Day ticks were filtered by PARITY, so on a 1 2 3 4 5 7 9 11 15 18 22 25 schedule the axis appeared
  to stop at day 15 while three more sessions were plotted past it. Now thinned by index.
* The engagement timeline now names **WORKING** and **STOPPED**, draws the BACKDATED cut rather than
  where the trailing mean noticed, marks the reference trials the gate actually judged
  (close_L / close_center only), and titles which arm fired. The shading was already the right gate;
  what was missing was saying so.
