## The headline finding

**`wfield.hemodynamic_correction` removes drift with a ZERO-PHASE (acausal) 0.1 Hz filter, and the
filtered channel becomes `SVTcorr`.** A zero-phase filter's impulse response is symmetric in time
(measured on this filter: −0.496 before an impulse, −0.496 after), so each position-specific POST-cue
response casts a sign-flipped shadow BACKWARDS into the pre-cue window. A decoder does not care about
sign, so that shadow read as pre-cue position information.

**This is the upstream Churchland/wfield method, not a local bug**, and the artifact class is published
(van Driel, Olivers & Fahrenfort 2021, *J Neurosci Methods*). Our exposure is worse than theirs because
we DECODE a condition label from a window PRECEDING the condition-specific response; their headline
analyses are encoding models with lagged regressors that absorb the distortion into the fitted kernel.

**ADOPTED FIX: `meegkit_hpfit`, polynomial order 10.** Keep the 0.1 Hz high-pass for the COEFFICIENT
FIT (that is what it is for — `rcoeffs` is one scalar per pixel and needs the hemodynamic band to
dominate the fit), and replace it for the OUTPUT with de Cheveigné robust polynomial detrending on a
mask that excludes the whole trial. 36 curated sessions: pre-cue 0.486 → 0.352, **post-cue 0.684 →
0.759** (better), shadow signature gone (negative pre/post correlation in 30/36 → 2/36).

**What survives:** pre-cue position information is real, significant in **35/36 sessions**, at ~72% of
the published effect. PS92 0.225, PS93 0.349, PS94 **0.500**, PS95 0.334 (chance 0.167, empirical null
0.137–0.147). PS94 essentially untouched; PS92 the one substantially inflated.

**Terminology changed** to "pre-cue position information", not "maintained motor plan" — the spout
arrives ~3 s before the cue, so a sustained sensory response and a held intention are temporally
coextensive and this design cannot separate them. It does not need to: a pre-cue position signal that
changes post-stroke is the readout either way.

## Other findings, 13–14 Aug

**Decoder windows and alignment** (`DECISIONS.md` has the tables):
* **Do NOT extend the cue window to 3 s.** PS94/PS95 decline monotonically; PS93 far_L — the
  motivation — does not improve at any length. `cue_post_s` stays 2.0.
* **Lick alignment beats cue alignment in 4/4 animals** (PS92 +0.075, PS93 +0.071, PS94 +0.016,
  PS95 +0.032), and recovers PS93 far_L from 0.436 → 0.556. The limiting factor is RT VARIABILITY, not
  RT magnitude — a jittered response smears under cue alignment and a longer window adds noise without
  unsmearing it.
* **Pre-cue: 4 × 0.5 s sub-bins beat the 2 s mean** (+0.032, 14/16) on corrected data — the reverse of
  what the artifact-laden data showed. 8 × 0.25 s is worse; 250 ms over-slices.
* **Pre-lick instead of pre-cue: NO** — RT differs by position (0.128–0.384 s), so a pre-lick window
  admits a position-dependent slice of the post-cue response and would partly decode reaction time. As
  an ADDITIONAL movement-preparation analysis: yes, with RT matched or regressed out.

**Joint basis:** `variance_captured` is NOT a sufficient health check — 8/12 reads 98.9–99.4% while
carrying a −0.079 mean projection penalty (PS92 −0.172). Every post-stroke session will be a projected
day, so that cost must be calibrated on held-out pre-stroke days, or Allen-ROI used as the primary
readout since it involves no projection.

## Cautions that have bitten already

* **`nightly_figs` used to exit 0 with an empty deck.** It now returns non-zero, but check the
  "figures placed / missing" line regardless.
* **Never run `python -m wfield_local.preprocess_deck`** — its `build_decks` prunes sibling decks. Call
  `build_deck` per animal.
* **Pipe background jobs to a file, not through `grep`** without `--line-buffered`: output buffers and
  the job looks dead. This cost a nearly-complete job earlier.
* **Do not tune a window or parameter on the outcome it is judged by.** The 60 s detrend window sat
  exactly on the position-block timescale (57–121 s) and removed the most signal of any setting tested;
  the "code builds toward the cue" asymmetry (+0.246) was the filter shadow and vanished (−0.040) once
  corrected.
* **A claim asserted before it is tested is the recurring failure mode here.** Six corrections in two
  days, most surfaced by Priya's questions rather than by my own checks. Test first, then write it down.
