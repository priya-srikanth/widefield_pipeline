## 1. WHAT WAS BUILT

### `epoch_15g` — the cross-epoch TRANSFER MATRIX (`scripts/rest_migration/transfer_arms.py`)

Train a decoder on epoch A, score it on epoch B, for every ordered pair, in the shared joint
LocaNMF basis. Four windows: **ENL** (precue/working), **cue** (cue/working), **lick** (lick/lick),
**rest** (restdock05). Core in `wfield_local/transfer_matrix.py`, 18 guards.

**THE TOP ROW IS NOT NEW** — `train pre -> test post` is what the existing frozen decoders already
report. Rows 2-4 are new, and `chronic -> pre` is the only cell where ROTATION and ADDITION predict
different things. Differences from the published families in the shared row: training size
block-matched in EVERY cell (as `5rm`, not `5r`), and pooled balanced accuracy rather than
per-position. **The block-permutation null is NOT a difference** — `decode_ci.frozen_ci` already
uses it.

**Result 1 — ADDITION IS REJECTED IN ALL FOUR WINDOWS.** Raw above-chance asymmetry
(`chronic->pre` minus `pre->chronic`), PS92 / PS93 / PS95:

| arm | asymmetry | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| ENL | −0.061 / −0.004 / −0.079 | SPLIT (2/3 material) |
| cue | −0.095 / −0.088 / −0.021 | SYMMETRIC |
| lick | −0.050 / −0.020 / −0.007 | SYMMETRIC |
| rest | +0.002 / +0.021 / +0.015 | SYMMETRIC |

Addition predicts a LARGE POSITIVE asymmetry. None is material in either direction. The transfer is
symmetric — a **partial rotation**, retaining 0.79/0.78 of ceiling for cue and 0.81/0.82 for lick,
0.64/0.57 for ENL, 0.35/0.53 for rest.

**Result 2 — EVERY WINDOW ENDS ABOVE ITS PRE-STROKE CEILING.** Own-epoch above-chance:
ENL 0.29 → 0.25 → 0.29 → **0.33**; cue 0.62 → 0.39 → 0.59 → **0.70**; lick 0.71 → 0.57 → 0.67 →
**0.74**. Positive in **9 of 9** arm-animal pairs.

**The practice confound is measured and inert.** Across the ~70-day PRE-STROKE span — same
practice, no lesion — decodability does not rise with day: mean slope **−0.009 per 30 days**.
Practice predicts a slight DECLINE over the stroke-to-chronic interval, against gains of +0.02 to
+0.20.

### Significance — 200 draws, and what it does NOT license

`epoch_15h_rotation_regions.csv` carries two per-cell p values: `cos_p_vs_noise` (one-sided, does
the readout turn further than estimation noise) and `gain_resid_p` (two-sided, on the RESIDUAL
log-gain so the epoch-wide scale cancels). 240 cells = arm x contrast x animal x position.

| | |
|---|---|
| cosine p < 0.05, UNCORRECTED | **153 / 240 (64%)** |
| residual-gain p < 0.05 | 64 / 240 (27%) |
| surviving Bonferroni | **0** |

**THE ZERO IS NOT A RESULT — IT IS THE DRAW COUNT, AND THE FAMILY WAS WRONG TOO.**

*240 = 4 windows × 3 contrasts × 6 positions × animals-with-data* (18 acute — PS95 has one acute
session; 24 subacute — all four; 18 chronic — PS94 has none). **The ANIMAL axis does not belong in
the correction family**: animals are REPLICATES of one hypothesis, not separate hypotheses.
Correcting across them asks "does far-contra rotate acutely in cue" as four different questions.
**The honest family is 72** (window × contrast × position).

It barely helps, because the draw count binds either way — the empirical floor at 200 draws is
`1/201 = 0.00498`:

| family | n | alpha | reachable at 200 draws | draws needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| × animals (as first reported) | 240 | 0.00021 | no | >= 4,799 |
| **window × contrast × position** | **72** | 0.00069 | no | **>= 1,439** |
| one window | 18 | 0.00278 | no | >= 359 |
| one window, one epoch | 6 | 0.00833 | **yes** | — |

So ~1,440 draws (~2.5 h), not the ~5,000 (~8 h) first implied. **Never report "nothing survived
correction" from this run** — no cell CAN survive at 200 draws whatever the effect size.

**AND BONFERRONI IS THE WRONG CORRECTION ANYWAY — USE A PERMUTATION MAX-STATISTIC.**

The six positions within a window are NOT independent. `LogisticRegression` is multinomial, so the
class coefficients are identified only up to a constant shift across classes: the six patterns are
coupled by construction. That is the same coupling that makes MEANref unusable as a deficit
measure. Bonferroni assumes independence and is therefore conservative by an unknown amount here.

**THE FIX, and the machinery already exists in this repo.** The map families correct with
`maxstat` (`epoch_15r_*_stats.csv` carries a `correction` column reading `maxstat`). The same
construction applies to the cosines:

1. For each permutation draw, compute the cosine for **all cells in the family** from the SAME
   split-half draw.
2. Record the draw's **most extreme** statistic across the family (the max |z|, or the min cosine).
3. The family-wise null is the distribution of those per-draw maxima.
4. A cell is significant at FWE 0.05 if it beats the 95th percentile of that distribution.

This respects the coupling — correlated cells produce correlated draws, so the max distribution is
narrower than the independence assumption implies — and it needs **no more draws than the per-cell
test**, because the family-wise null is built from the draws already being run. It is strictly
better than Bonferroni here, not merely different.

**THE FAMILY IS window × contrast × position = 72, NOT 240.** Animals are REPLICATES and belong in
the replication count, not the correction. See the table above.

**IMPLEMENTATION NOTE:** `null_delta` currently returns per-position draws independently. A
max-statistic version needs the per-draw values held together across positions — the draws are
already synchronised (same `seed_ns|null{d}`, same split), so it is a restructuring of what is
returned, not extra computation.

**PER-ANIMAL REPLICATION IS THE DEFENSIBLE SUMMARY**, and it is consistent — animals with >= 3 of 6
positions significant:

| arm | acute | subacute | chronic |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENL | PS92,93,94 | PS92,93,94 | PS92,93 |
| cue | PS92,93,94 | **PS92,93,94,95** | PS92,93,95 |
| lick | PS92,93,94 | PS92,93,95 | PS92,93 |
| rest | PS92,93,94 | PS92,93,94 | PS92,93 |

**STILL MISSING: an across-animal statistic.** These are per-cell tests counted by animal, not a
cohort test. The project's standard is the nested animals->sessions bootstrap; that has not been
applied to the cosines.

## 2. THE FRAMING QUESTION — AND WHAT 15g/15h CANNOT ANSWER

Priya, 2026-09-17, and it is the single most clarifying thing in this arm:

> *"there are two ways to approach this analysis: (1) comparing each position's response across
> epochs and (2) comparing a position's response RELATIVE TO OTHER POSITIONS' across epochs."*

**15g and 15h are entirely framing (2).** `LogisticRegression` is multinomial, so `coef_[p]` is the
direction separating position *p* from the other five; `A = Cov(X)·β_p` inherits it; the cosine
compares that CONTRAST's direction across epochs; the residual gain explicitly subtracts the
cross-position mean.

**The blind spot follows: a change affecting all six positions equally is INVISIBLE to 15g/15h.**
If the lesion halved every position's response uniformly, the discriminative directions could be
untouched — cosine at ceiling, residual gain zero — while framing (1) reported a 50% loss.

Framing (1) lives in the ENCODER and the map families. `STATUS_2026-09-12` already reasons about
this split: **MEANref couples positions** ("one losing drive hands the other five an unearned
increase") and is flagged NOT a deficit measure; **RESTref** (then called QUIETref) keeps them
independent.

### GAIN IS NOT A RESPONSE AMPLITUDE — measured, r = +0.039

`gain = ||A||_epoch / ||A||_pre`. Correlated against the evoked family's amplitude ratio over 60
(animal × position × epoch) cells: **r = +0.039**. They are unrelated, for structural reasons:

* evoked amplitude is FIRST-moment (mean ΔF/F vs a baseline); `||Cov(X)·β||` is SECOND-moment
* `β` is one-vs-rest, so gain asks how *p* differs FROM THE OTHER FIVE
* gain carries the epoch-wide `Cov(X)` scale

**Decomposition:** splitting each epoch's six gains into a common factor and a position-specific
residual gives mean |common| 0.397 against mean sd(residual) 0.356 — **the epoch-wide factor is
1.11× the position-specific spread**. So roughly half of any gain number is how loud the epoch was.
**Read gain against the other five positions in the same epoch, never against 1.0.** That is why the
gain test is on the RESIDUAL log-gain.

So three distinct quantities, each needing its own name:

| quantity | measures | lives in |
|---|---|---|
| evoked amplitude | response size to that position | `epoch_14` / `evoked_amplitude` / RESTref |
| **gain** | distinctiveness vs the other five, × epoch scale | `epoch_15h` |
| **cosine** | direction of the readout, scale removed | `epoch_15h` |

## 3. METHOD TRAPS FOUND AND FIXED — read before building any similar contrast

1. **NEVER COMPARE TWO `retained` RATIOS ACROSS DIRECTIONS.** They have different denominators, so
   `retained(B->A) − retained(A->B) = r·(1/ceil_A − 1/ceil_B)` is nonzero for symmetric raw data.
   Measured: raw transfer symmetric in 3/3 animals, normalised asymmetry +0.404 / +0.441 / −0.001,
   predicted +0.432 / +0.331 / +0.053 from the ceilings alone. **The first version printed
   "consistent with ADDITION" and was reporting its own denominators.** Direction contrasts are
   taken on RAW above-chance. In DECISIONS.md; generalises to any "fraction retained" comparison.
2. **THE RAW |change| MAP LOCALISES THE BASIS, NOT THE LESION.** It correlates with a null built
   from two PRE-STROKE halves at **r = +0.83 / +0.81**. Component footprint mass spans **67×**, so a
   component-space unit norm projects large midline components to large pixel mass whatever their
   coefficient — retrosplenial topped every panel, including the null. Each component is now scored
   against ITS OWN split-half null.
3. **A COSINE NEEDS A NOISE CEILING, AND THE CEILING NEEDS MATCHING TOO.** Absent, 0.64 could be a
   large rotation or a noisy estimate of none. A PRE-ONLY ceiling was then systematically
   pessimistic — its models saw 5-6 sessions against the epoch models' 11 — and PS92's ENL ceiling
   came out at +0.260 while its cosines were 0.410-0.437, i.e. ABOVE their own ceiling. Now each
   epoch's own reliability is measured and the cosine is attenuation-corrected in both:
   `cos / sqrt(rel_pre · rel_epoch)`.
4. **AVERAGING OVER POSITIONS HID THE RESULT.** The cue arm's far-contra cosine of −0.042 sat among
   five values near +0.6. Everything is per position now, in the canonical Near Ipsi → Far Contra
   order (`CONF_LABELS`; `POSITION_NAMES` puts close_CENTER at 0, so sorting by code transposes the
   figure against every neighbour).
5. **THE MAP AND THE COSINE ARE DIFFERENT QUANTITIES.** The map is scale-FREE (each component
   against its own noise); the cosine is scale-WEIGHTED. PS93 cue acute: far_center has mean |z|
   **11.1** with 20% of its change in the top-weight components and a cosine of 0.37, while far_R
   has mean |z| 3.7, 37% in the top weights, and a cosine of **−0.29**. **Judge rotation by the
   bars, not by map brightness.**
6. **COLOUR SEMANTICS ARE RESERVED.** `tab:blue/red/orange/green` ALREADY mean PS92/93/94/95 —
   that is `configs/animals.yaml`, not a local choice. Windows take brown/teal/olive; epochs
   delegate to `epoch_figures.EPOCH_GREY`, which already owned the grey ramp.
7. **MASK THE MAPS.** `beta_maps.stat_mask` removes olfactory bulbs and the glue/window edge, where
   `U` is smallest and the Allen warp least constrained.

## A2. NEW: `epoch_15j` — THE REST BASELINE *DOES* DRIFT ACROSS EPOCHS

`scripts/rest_migration/rest_baseline_epoch_drift.py`. This is the narrow question §0b left open, and

**6 of 11 animal-epoch cells significant, 3 of 4 animals. Shifts 7–43% of the evoked signal (median
~0.15)** — ~5x `uniform_shift_check`'s 3.1% and far below the retired QUIET-era ~52%. All three are

| animal | acute | subacute | chronic |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS92 | 0.139 | **0.279*** | **0.342*** |
| PS93 | **0.148*** | **0.126*** | **0.166*** |
| PS94 | 0.165 | **0.432*** | (no chronic) |

**UNITS:** dimensionless — `‖mean_E(b/k) − mean_pre(b/k)‖`, each session's baseline divided by its
OWN evoked norm `k` (which is what cancels cross-day MULTIPLICATIVE scaling). It is a whole-map
vector norm, and it is an **UPPER BOUND on the amplitude bias**, attained only if the shift is
aligned with the evoked pattern; orthogonal shifts cost ~r²/2 (0.15 → ~1%). So 0.15 means "between
~1% and ~15%".

**Acute is the LEAST affected epoch** despite carrying the largest deficit — so this does not simply
track the lesion. PS95 is null throughout.

**THE COSINE CARRIES NO p AND MUST NOT BE READ AGAINST ZERO.** `d` holds `−mean_pre(bn)` and
`e_ref = mean_pre(post/k) − mean_pre(bn)` holds the same vector, same sign → biased positive before
any drift exists. Two nulls were tried; the algebra defeats both (a subset of pre CANCELS the term:
4 of 20 gives `d = 0.8(mean_ga − mean_gb)`). A pre-drawn null is ANTI-CONSERVATIVE. Ships as
`cos_with_evoked_biased`. Fixing it needs `e_ref` from HELD-OUT pre sessions; 12 pre vs 6 acute does
not leave room. **Open.**

## A6. THE ROTATION-vs-GAIN RESULT, and a per-cell claim I got wrong

Measured on the completed 2026-09-17 run (`epoch_15h_rotation_regions.csv`, 264 unique
arm x contrast x animal x position cells). **These are `MIN_IN_MASK_FRAC = 0.5` numbers**; the 0.75
comparison is the `_mf075` set.

### The conclusion: the code ROTATES while getting STRONGER, and the two are independent

| arm | acute | subacute | chronic |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENL | cos +0.33 / ceil +0.52, gain 1.04 | +0.39 / +0.52, gain 0.95 | +0.36 / +0.41, gain **1.32** |
| cue | +0.39 / +0.81, gain 1.09 | +0.65 / +0.81, gain 1.11 | +0.48 / +0.78, gain **1.64** |
| lick | +0.69 / +0.83, gain 1.05 | +0.72 / +0.83, gain 1.03 | +0.63 / +0.80, gain **1.28** |
| rest | +0.12 / +0.47, gain **1.73** | +0.22 / +0.47, gain **2.67** | +0.22 / +0.48, gain **1.57** |

1. **Every PANEL MEAN sits below its noise ceiling**, in all 12 arm x epoch combinations, so the
   readout direction genuinely turns. Ordering is the familiar one — lick most preserved, then cue,
   then ENL, then rest (0.12 against a 0.47 ceiling, nearly total).
2. **Gain is >= 1 almost everywhere and RISES INTO CHRONIC.** The code is not fading; it becomes
   MORE distinctive while pointing elsewhere. Same fact `15g` reports from accuracy alone (every
   window ends above its pre-stroke ceiling), reached through an unrelated quantity.
3. **The two axes are near-orthogonal: r = -0.142** between cosine and log-gain over 264 cells, and
   all four quadrants are populated (rotated+strengthened 78, preserved+strengthened 78,
   preserved+weakened 67, rotated+weakened 41). If rotation and weakening were one phenomenon the
   diagonal quadrants would be empty. They are not.

So "did the lesion degrade the code or move it" answers **MOVED, AND AMPLIFIED** — most in rest,
least in lick.

### THE CORRECTION: 24% OF CELLS SIT ABOVE THEIR OWN CEILING

I wrote "every cosine sits below its noise ceiling". **That is a PANEL-MEAN claim stated per cell,
and it is false per cell.** Priya caught it from the figure, where the ENL and cue chronic panels
plainly carry points at ~+0.85-0.9 above their ceiling lines.

**64 of 264 cells (24%) are above their own ceiling**, and evenly spread — 3-8 per panel, no arm or
epoch concentrating them:

| arm | acute | subacute | chronic |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENL | 5/24 | 8/24 | 7/18 |
| cue | 3/24 | 5/24 | 4/18 |
| lick | 7/24 | 3/24 | 4/18 |
| rest | 6/24 | 6/24 | 6/18 |

**THE FLATNESS IS THE INTERPRETATION.** A cosine above its ceiling means the epoch pattern matches
pre BETTER than two halves of pre match each other, which is only possible by chance — the ceiling
is the upper bound on what is measurable. ~24% at a ceiling built from split-half reliability is
about what noise in both estimates predicts. Concentrated in one arm it would mean the ceiling was
mis-estimated there; spread evenly it simply marks the resolution limit.

**PRACTICAL RULE: a cell at or above its ceiling carries NO rotation claim in either direction.** It
is not "preserved", it is UNMEASURABLE. The rotation result rests on the cells well below the
ceiling — which is why `cos_attenuation_corrected` and `cos_p_vs_noise` are in the CSV and the raw
cosine alone is not the statistic.

### AND GAIN >= 1 DOES NOT MEAN BIGGER RESPONSES

Repeated here because the plane invites the misreading. Gain is `||A||_epoch / ||A||_pre` on the
DECODER pattern: second-moment, one-vs-rest, and carrying the epoch-wide `Cov(X)` scale. Correlated
against evoked amplitude at **r = +0.039** over 60 cells, and roughly HALF of any gain number is how
loud the epoch was (common factor 1.11x the position-specific spread). It means MORE DISTINCTIVE
FROM THE OTHER FIVE POSITIONS, not "responded more" — which is why the test runs on residual
log-gain and why the deficit must be read from the REST-referenced maps and the encoder instead.
